About Us

Where Tashkent comes to game

Energy Gaming Huvaydo is a gaming café in the Beruniy area of Tashkent. We started it because the city was missing a spot with proper hardware and a normal price.

Whether you're climbing ranked, prepping for a tournament, or just queueing with the squad, the PCs here are tuned for the games you actually play.

We run local tournaments on weekends, host LAN parties, and try to keep the Tashkent esports scene moving. Come hang out.
